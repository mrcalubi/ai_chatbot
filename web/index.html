    How to use the AI Chatbot

    Workflow

    1. Upload PDFs (watch the indexing spinner finish).
    2. Ask your question. Toggle Strict for quote-only answers.
    3. Open Evidence to see page and snippet.
    4. Use the trash icon to remove a single file, or Clear All.
    Years boost retrieval. Include years like 2024 2025 2026.

    Strict mode

    • ON → Answer is only short verbatim quotes from your PDFs. No paraphrase.
    • OFF → Summary allowed. Any numbers not found in sources are redacted as [unverified].
    • Toggle per question via the Strict checkbox.

    Power keywords (boosts retrieval)

    capex funding financing debt equity cash flow guidance capacity EBITDA revenue dividends net debt D&A working capital

    Use company names/tickers + years for precision, e.g. DGC capex 2024–2026.

    Special modes

    • FULL: prefix → deterministic literal matches (no AI).
      FULL: capex 2024
    • Exports (for analysts)
      • /export/full — full plain text (all docs or by ?doc=)
      • /export/matches?q=… — raw top-k chunks
      • /search/doc — literal hits in one PDF
      • /chunks/doc — list of all chunks
